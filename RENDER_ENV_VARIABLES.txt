===========================================
ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES FOR RENDER
===========================================

Copy and paste these into Render's "Environment Variables" section:

1. NODE_ENV
   Value: production

2. MONGODB_URI
   Value: mongodb+srv://YOUR_USERNAME:YOUR_PASSWORD@cluster0.xxxxx.mongodb.net/chanakya-academy?retryWrites=true&w=majority
   ⚠️ Replace with your actual MongoDB Atlas connection string

3. NEXTAUTH_URL
   Value: https://the-chanakya-academy.onrender.com
   ⚠️ Update this AFTER first deployment with your actual Render URL

4. NEXTAUTH_SECRET
   Value: [GENERATE_A_RANDOM_32_CHARACTER_STRING]
   ⚠️ Generate at: https://generate-secret.vercel.app/32

5. NEXT_PUBLIC_FRONTEND_URL (Optional but recommended)
   Value: https://the-chanakya-academy.vercel.app
   ⚠️ This is your Vercel frontend URL for CORS configuration
   ⚠️ Update with your actual Vercel frontend URL
